The Problem

When You Don't Know Where The Water's Going

A lot of calls start the same way. Someone shuts off every fixture in the house and the water meter is still moving, or a water bill climbs for no clear reason and there is no puddle or wet spot to point to. That is exactly what leak detection is for. Instead of guessing at a wall or digging up the yard, we use equipment built to find where water is actually escaping the system, then bring you a real repair plan instead of a demolition bill.

Signs To Watch For

Signs You Have A Hidden Leak

Rising Water Bill

A bill that jumps with no new usage to explain it usually means water is going somewhere you can't see.

Meter Still Spins

Shut off every fixture in the house. If the meter dial keeps moving, water is leaving the system somewhere between the meter and your fixtures.

Warm Spot On The Floor

A patch of flooring that stays warmer than the rest of the room, especially over a hot water line, points to a slab leak.

Recurring Stain Or Damp Spot

A ceiling or wall stain that keeps coming back after painting over it usually means the source is still active.

Meter Still Moving?

That's the clearest sign of a hidden leak. We can start locating it today.

How We Find It

Non-Invasive Equipment We Actually Use

We start with electronic listening equipment, ground microphones that pick up the acoustic signature of water moving under pressure through a cracked line. For leaks on hot water lines, a thermal imaging camera can spot the temperature difference on a slab or wall surface before anything gets opened up. A pressure isolation test at the meter tells us whether the leak sits on the service line coming into the house, the main distribution lines, or a single fixture supply. None of this replaces a visual check where one is possible, but it narrows the search to one access point in most cases instead of several exploratory cuts.

Safety Note

What To Do While We Confirm The Leak

Our diagnostic approach follows standard practice under the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), the base code California adopts statewide for repair work once a leak is confirmed. A couple of things worth doing in the meantime:

Shut Off The Main If Water Is Visible

If you can see active water rather than just a moving meter, shutting off the main valve limits damage until a technician arrives.

Keep Electrical Items Away From Wet Areas

Standing water near outlets or appliances is a real shock hazard, not just a plumbing inconvenience. Stay clear until the area is dry.

Local Cause

Why So Many Murrieta Leak Calls Trace Back To The Slab

Murrieta sits on expansive clay soil that swells during wet winters and shrinks back through the long dry summer. That seasonal movement puts real mechanical stress on the joints and connections in copper lines buried under slab-on-grade foundations. A large share of the city's housing stock was built during the 1990-2006 boom, so a lot of that original copper is aging at the same time. Hard water adds a separate factor. Mineral buildup inside older copper can accelerate internal corrosion, a different mechanism from soil-driven joint stress, but it shows up in the same part of town for the same underlying reason: aging pipe under a home built in that era. When a leak turns out to be under the slab specifically, that becomes a slab leak repair rather than a standard fix.

FAQs

Common Questions

Why is my water bill high but I don't see any leak?

Most hidden leaks in Murrieta are slab leaks or breaks in buried yard lines, both invisible without equipment. If your meter keeps moving with everything off, that's leak detection territory, not something to keep watching.

How long does leak detection take?

Most jobs are completed in one visit, usually one to three hours depending on how many areas need to be checked.

Will you have to cut into my slab or walls?

Our equipment is meant to narrow the location first so we open one spot instead of several. Some limited exploratory access is occasionally still needed to confirm the exact point before repair.

Does homeowner's insurance cover this?

Insurance sometimes covers sudden water damage caused by a leak, but usually not the cost of finding or fixing the leak itself. Check your specific policy before assuming coverage.

What if you can't pinpoint the leak in one visit?

It's uncommon, but if the meter test and inspection don't confirm a single source, we'll walk you through the next honest step instead of repeating the same test and charging again with no new plan.

Do you cover my part of Murrieta?

Yes. We cover all of Murrieta and the surrounding area, including Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Menifee, and Canyon Lake.
