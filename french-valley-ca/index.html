French Valley isn't a city, it's a census-designated place, meaning the Census Bureau tracks it as a distinct community even though it has never incorporated with its own city government. That hasn't stopped it from growing into the most populous CDP in Riverside County, with a population that jumped from roughly 23,000 in 2010 to over 35,000 by 2020. The name itself goes back to French grain farmers and sheepherders who worked this valley in the late 1800s, long before Interstate 15 or the housing booms that followed. Most of what's here today is considerably newer than that history suggests, with subdivisions built mainly in the 2000s and 2010s filling in what was open ranch land a generation ago. French Valley Airport, a county-owned general aviation field right off Winchester Road, sits inside the community and gives the area a genuinely distinct identity even without its own city hall. For plumbing purposes, French Valley's newer average construction age means less of the original-copper fatigue driving slab leak calls elsewhere in Murrieta, though the same regional hard water still applies to water heaters and fixtures throughout the area.

Why French Valley Homes Call Us

Mostly Newer Construction

Most of French Valley's housing dates to the 2000s and 2010s, during the area's fastest growth period, so original-copper slab leak calls are less common here than in older parts of Murrieta.

Eastern Municipal Water District Supply

Most of French Valley draws water through Eastern Municipal Water District rather than the Rancho California Water District that covers much of Murrieta, with its own pressure and hardness profile.

Airport-Adjacent Properties

Homes and commercial buildings near French Valley Airport along Sky Canyon Drive sometimes have plumbing tied to commercial-grade fixtures or older hangar-area infrastructure that a standard residential repair doesn't always cover.

Rapid, Recent Growth

With the population up more than 50% since 2010, new-construction plumbing work and warranty-adjacent repairs sit alongside standard service calls.

FAQs

Questions From French Valley

Is French Valley a separate city from Murrieta?

No. French Valley is an unincorporated census-designated place, not an incorporated city, which is why it has no city hall or separate building department of its own. It sits immediately east of Murrieta and shares part of its ZIP code with Murrieta itself.

Do you cover every ZIP code in French Valley?

Yes. French Valley spans ZIP codes 92563 and 92596, the same 92563 code that covers a large part of Murrieta itself, plus 92596 toward Winchester.

Is emergency service available in French Valley?

Yes, our emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, for French Valley calls, including weekends and holidays.

How far is French Valley from your Murrieta base?

About 5 to 6 miles, roughly a 10 to 15 minute drive, so it runs on the same daily routes as the rest of our Murrieta coverage rather than as a separate trip.

Does French Valley have its own water district?

Most of French Valley is served by Eastern Municipal Water District, which is different from the Rancho California Water District that covers much of Murrieta, so exact hardness and pressure can vary somewhat by street.

Is French Valley mostly newer construction?

Largely yes. The area's population has grown from about 23,000 in 2010 to over 35,000 in 2020, and a lot of that growth is recent subdivisions, though some older ranch properties remain scattered throughout.

Do you service properties near French Valley Airport?

Yes, both residential and commercial properties near the airport corridor along Sky Canyon Drive and Winchester Road are inside our regular coverage area.
