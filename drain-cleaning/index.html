The Problem

When A Plunger Isn't Cutting It Anymore

Most clogs start small and get pushed back with a plunger or a bottle of drain cleaner, until the day neither one works. At that point the blockage is usually further down the line than either tool can reach, and that's exactly where professional snaking and camera inspection earn their keep instead of another trip to the store.

Signs To Watch For

Signs You Need Professional Drain Cleaning

Water Backs Up Instead Of Draining

Standing water that won't go down, or comes back up somewhere else, means the blockage is beyond what a plunger can reach.

Multiple Drains Clogged At Once

A kitchen sink and a bathroom drain acting up on the same day usually points to a main line issue, not two separate clogs.

Gurgling Sounds From Other Drains

Air trapped behind a partial blockage often gurgles in a different fixture when something else drains.

The Same Clog Keeps Coming Back

A drain that clogs again a few weeks after snaking usually has a structural cause, like root intrusion, not just buildup.

How We Find It

Snaking, Hydro Jetting, And Camera Verification

For straightforward clogs, mechanical snaking physically breaks up or retrieves the blockage. For recurring or stubborn clogs, hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full diameter of the pipe rather than just punching a hole through the buildup. On a repeat offender or a suspected main line issue, we run a camera down the line to actually see the cause, root intrusion, a bellied section of pipe, or grease buildup, instead of guessing from the symptoms alone.

Before We Arrive

What To Do Before We Arrive

If a drain is actively backing up:

Stop Using The Affected Fixtures

Running more water into a backed-up line risks an overflow. Hold off until we've had a look.

Skip The Chemical Drain Cleaner

It can damage older pipe and creates a real safety hazard for anyone who has to work on the line afterward.

Local Cause

Why Root Intrusion Shows Up On Older Murrieta Lines

Homes built during Murrieta's 1990-2006 growth wave were typically landscaped with fast-growing shade trees planted near the same route as the sewer lateral running to the street. Two to three decades later, those trees have mature root systems, and older laterals, especially ABS or clay sections, are exactly the kind of pipe root intrusion targets at any small crack or joint gap. That's the most common reason a drain that seemed fine for years suddenly starts backing up on a predictable schedule.

FAQs

Common Questions

Why does my drain clog again a few weeks after cleaning?

A repeat clog in the same spot usually points to a structural cause like root intrusion or a bellied pipe section, which is exactly what a camera inspection is for.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

Generally yes for pipe in reasonable condition, but we camera-check first on older or unknown pipe before jetting at full pressure.

Can you tell if it's root intrusion without digging?

Yes, a camera run down the line shows root intrusion, cracks, or bellied sections without any digging.

Do you offer emergency drain cleaning?

Yes, our line is answered 24/7 for active backups.

What if multiple drains are backing up at the same time?

That's usually a main line issue rather than separate clogs. See our sewer line repair page, or just call and describe what you're seeing.

Do you serve areas outside Murrieta?

Yes, including Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Menifee, and Canyon Lake.
