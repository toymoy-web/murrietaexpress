The Problem

See The Problem Before Paying To Fix It

A camera inspection turns a guess into an actual answer. Instead of assuming a repeat clog means root intrusion, or assuming a soggy yard patch means a major collapse, we run a camera down the line and show you exactly what's there. That means seeing where the problem is and how bad it actually is, before any repair conversation starts.

Signs To Watch For

When A Camera Inspection Makes Sense

Recurring Clogs In The Same Line

A drain that clogs again a few weeks after snaking usually has a cause worth actually seeing, not guessing at again.

Buying Or Selling An Older Home

A sewer lateral is one of the most expensive things to replace and one of the easiest to overlook during a standard home inspection.

Before Committing To A Repair Quote

Confirming the real cause and location before agreeing to a repair protects you from paying for more excavation than the problem actually needs.

Unexplained Wet Spots In The Yard

A soggy patch with no obvious cause is worth checking before it becomes a full sinkhole.

Buying An Older Murrieta Home?

A camera inspection before closing can save a costly surprise later.

How We Find It

How The Inspection Works

We feed a waterproof camera down the line from an existing cleanout or a fixture drain, recording footage as it goes and tracking the camera's position so we can tell you exactly how far from the house any problem sits. On request, we can provide the recorded footage itself, useful for a real estate transaction or simply for your own records, along with a plain-language explanation of what the footage actually shows.

Local Cause

Why This Matters More On Certain Murrieta Homes

Homes built during Murrieta's 1990-2006 growth wave are old enough now that original ABS or clay sewer laterals are a real, common inspection finding, especially where mature landscaping trees have had two to three decades to grow root systems near the line. A camera inspection is the only way to tell whether a specific home's line is still sound or already showing early root intrusion, rather than assuming based on the neighborhood's general age alone. If root intrusion is confirmed, the follow-up is often hydro jetting to clear it before deciding on any structural repair.

Common Questions

Do you provide video footage I can keep?

Yes, recorded footage is available on request, useful for real estate transactions, insurance documentation, or your own records.

Is the inspection fee applied toward a repair?

In most cases, yes, if you move forward with us for the actual repair.

Can this be done before I buy a home?

Yes, a pre-purchase sewer camera inspection is one of the more overlooked but valuable inspections on an older home.

How do you access the line for the camera?

Usually through an existing cleanout. If one doesn't exist or can't be located, we'll discuss the best access point before starting.

What if the camera finds a problem?

We'll show you the footage, explain what it means in plain language, and discuss real repair options, including whether trenchless methods apply.

Do you serve areas outside Murrieta?

Yes, including Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Menifee, and Canyon Lake.
