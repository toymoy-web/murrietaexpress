The Problem

Why Tankless Units Need More Attention Here Than Elsewhere

A tankless water heater doesn't store water, but it does run every drop through a heat exchanger, and Murrieta's hard water, commonly 10 to 25 grains per gallon, leaves mineral scale inside that exchanger faster than it would in a soft-water area. Skipping annual descaling here isn't a minor shortcut, it's the single most common reason we see tankless units underperforming or throwing error codes years before they should.

Signs To Watch For

Signs Your Tankless Unit Needs Service

Fluctuating Water Temperature

Temperature that swings during a shower instead of staying steady often means scale buildup is affecting the heat exchanger's response.

An Error Code On The Display

Modern tankless units display specific error codes rather than just failing silently, and reading that code correctly usually points straight to the cause.

Reduced Flow Rate

A noticeable drop in how much hot water the unit can deliver at once is a common scale-related symptom.

It's Been A Year Or More Since The Last Descale

Manufacturers generally recommend annual descaling in hard water areas, and Murrieta's water qualifies.

How We Find It

Descaling And Diagnostic Process

We flush the heat exchanger with a descaling solution circulated through the unit's isolation valves, a process most tankless manufacturers recommend annually in hard water areas like this one, consistent with manufacturer specifications and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) requirements for the unit's installation. If a unit is displaying an error code, we identify what that specific code means for your unit's brand before doing anything, since a code often points to one of a few likely causes rather than requiring a full teardown to diagnose.

Local Cause

Hard Water And Tankless Units, A Direct Relationship

The same 10-25 grains per gallon hardness that affects tank water heaters and toilets across Murrieta hits tankless units even harder, since every gallon of water passes directly across the heat exchanger's surface. A whole-home water softener meaningfully extends the interval between necessary descaling and reduces the odds of an early component failure, on top of everything else it does for the rest of the home's plumbing.

Maintenance Matters

With Annual Descaling vs. Without

Without Regular Descaling

  • Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger
  • Temperature fluctuation and reduced flow over time
  • Higher risk of early component failure
  • Error codes appear years earlier than they should

With Annual Descaling

  • Heat exchanger stays closer to factory efficiency
  • Consistent temperature and flow rate
  • Extends the unit's realistic service life
  • Catches minor issues before they become failures

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless unit be descaled in Murrieta?

Most manufacturers recommend annually in hard water areas, and Murrieta's water hardness qualifies.

What does an error code on my unit actually mean?

It depends on the brand and code, but it's usually a specific, identifiable issue rather than a mystery. We look it up for your exact model before doing anything.

Can I descale a tankless unit myself?

It's possible with the right isolation valves and solution, but incorrect concentration or circulation time can cause its own issues, so many homeowners prefer to have it done professionally.

Will a water softener really help my tankless unit?

Yes, meaningfully. Reducing the mineral load extends the interval between necessary descaling and reduces long-term component wear.

Is my tankless unit still under warranty for this issue?

It depends on the manufacturer and the specific failure. We can help you determine that before recommending an out-of-pocket repair.

Do you serve areas outside Murrieta?

Yes, including Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Menifee, and Canyon Lake.
