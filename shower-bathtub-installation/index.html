The Problem

Getting The Plumbing Right, Not Just The Fixture

A shower or tub installation is as much about the plumbing behind the wall as the fixture itself. Supply lines, drain connections, and valve placement all need to be right before tile or a surround ever goes up, which is why we handle the plumbing side of this job directly rather than just setting a fixture in place.

Signs To Watch For

Common Reasons For A New Installation

An Aging Or Damaged Tub Or Shower

Cracked fiberglass, worn enamel, or persistent leaks around an old unit are common reasons to replace rather than keep patching.

Wanting A Tub-To-Shower Conversion

Converting an underused tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most common bathroom updates we handle.

Accessibility Needs

A low-threshold or walk-in shower can make a real difference for mobility, and we handle those installations with the right slope and support.

A Full Bathroom Remodel

New shower or tub installation is often part of a larger bathroom update, coordinated with other fixture work.

How We Find It

Installation And Conversion Process

For a straight replacement, we remove the old unit, inspect the supply and drain connections behind it, and install the new fixture with any necessary valve or line updates. For a tub-to-shower conversion, we also handle the drain relocation and slope work a proper walk-in shower pan requires, coordinating with tile or surround installation as needed.

Local Cause

What We See Most In Murrieta Bathrooms

Homes from Murrieta's 1990-2006 construction wave often still have their original tub and shower fixtures, well past typical service life, and tub-to-shower conversions are a common request as households age in place or simply prefer a shower-only setup. Hard water buildup on original fixtures is also a common reason homeowners decide replacement makes more sense than another round of scrubbing. If the water supply valves behind the wall are original too, that's often addressed at the same time as a broader plumbing repair visit.

Tub vs. Shower

Keeping A Tub vs. Converting To A Shower

Keep Or Replace The Tub

  • Better for households with young children
  • Maintains resale flexibility for some buyers
  • Simpler like-for-like replacement
  • Uses more water per use than a shower

Convert To A Shower

  • Better accessibility for mobility needs
  • Often preferred layout for primary bathrooms
  • Requires drain relocation and slope work
  • Uses less water per use than a bath

FAQs

Common Questions

How long does a tub-to-shower conversion take?

Most conversions take 2 to 4 days depending on drain relocation needs and whether tile work is involved.

Do I need a permit for this?

Typically yes for plumbing changes like drain relocation, which we handle as part of the job.

Can you match my existing bathroom's plumbing rough-in?

In many cases yes, though older or nonstandard rough-ins occasionally require adjustment, which we'll flag honestly during the estimate.

Do you install walk-in or accessible showers?

Yes, including low-threshold designs with proper slope and support for accessibility needs.

Do you handle tile or surround installation too?

We handle the plumbing and fixture work directly and can coordinate with tile or surround installation as part of the project.

Do you serve areas outside Murrieta?

Yes, including Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Menifee, and Canyon Lake.
