About Copper Canyon

A Varied-Density Community In West Murrieta

Copper Canyon is named on the City of Murrieta's zoning map as the Copper Canyon Specific Plan (SP 9). The city's 1995 environmental record describes it as a varied-density master-planned residential development, mostly single-family housing, with parks, open space and an 18-hole golf club. Local listings place it in west Murrieta at the base of the Santa Rosa Plateau, and the community has its own namesake parks, including Upper and Lower Copper Canyon Park.

Real estate profiles describe both one- and two-story single-family homes and attached homes such as townhomes and condominiums, built across the late 1980s through the 2000s. Cole Canyon Elementary is listed within the community. Published sources do not give a precise boundary line, so we place Copper Canyon by its parks and neighborhood streets and confirm your address by phone.

Questions About Copper Canyon

Is Copper Canyon part of Murrieta?

Yes. The Copper Canyon Specific Plan (SP 9) appears on the City of Murrieta's zoning map, and local sources place the community in west Murrieta.

Do you service townhomes and condos in Copper Canyon?

Yes. For attached homes, check with your community management about shutoff access and repair rules, and we will work with you from there.

Why do Copper Canyon fixtures get scale buildup?

Hard water is common across southwest Riverside County and leaves mineral deposits on fixtures and inside water heaters. A softener or filtration system is one option we can discuss.

What should I do about a slow drain?

Try to catch it early. A slow drain often responds to cleaning, and if it keeps returning, a camera inspection shows what is actually in the line.

How do I schedule service in Copper Canyon?

Call (951) 467-1450 and let us know whether you have a detached home or a townhome. That tells us how to plan the visit.
