About Bear Creek

A Gated Golf Community In West Murrieta

Bear Creek is a private, guard-gated community built around Bear Creek Golf & Country Club, an 18-hole course designed by Jack Nicklaus that opened in 1982. Local real estate profiles place it in the foothills of west Murrieta, just east of La Cresta, with Clinton Keith Road and Bear Creek Drive as the anchor streets. Addresses here use Murrieta, CA 92562.

The housing is mostly custom homes on large lots, with condominiums concentrated toward the south end of the community and many properties facing the course. One local lender's neighborhood guide describes the homes as built between roughly the mid-1980s and the mid-2000s. Bear Creek's exact boundaries are not published in a single authoritative source, so we describe it by those anchor streets and ask for your address when you call.

What Larger Gated Properties Mean For Service

Gate Details Up Front

Because Bear Creek is guard-gated, give us your street address and any entry instructions when you call so the visit is not held up at the gate.

Longer Outdoor Runs

Homes on larger lots often have longer supply lines to hose bibs, irrigation and pool equipment, which is general plumbing information rather than a claim about any one property.

Homes Of Different Ages

With construction spread across about two decades, we look at what is actually installed in your home before recommending anything, and we never assume a particular pipe material.

Condos At The South End

If you live in a condominium, check with your building or community management about shutoff access and repair rules before work starts.

Questions About Bear Creek

Do you service the gated part of Bear Creek?

Yes, Bear Creek is part of our Murrieta service area. Give us the address and any gate instructions when you call, and let the guard house know to expect us if your community requires it.

What ZIP code is Bear Creek?

Bear Creek addresses use Murrieta, CA 92562. We recommend confirming service by address when you call, because neighboring areas such as La Cresta have their own character and street layouts.

Do larger lots change how a plumbing repair is done?

They can change the scope. Longer runs to outdoor spigots, irrigation and pool equipment mean more places a hidden leak can occur, so leak detection is often the first step before opening anything up.

Do you work on pool and spa plumbing in Bear Creek?

We handle pool leak detection and repair, plus the household plumbing that feeds pool areas. Describe what you are seeing and we will explain what we can do over the phone.

How do I schedule service in Bear Creek?

Call (951) 467-1450 and tell us where you are in the community. We will confirm the address, ask about the problem and talk through next steps.
