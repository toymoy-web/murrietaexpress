About Los Alamos Hills

Neighborhood Around The Sports Park

Los Alamos Hills is a Murrieta neighborhood with ZIP code 92563, surrounded by the Murrieta Hogbacks. The roughly 40-acre Los Alamos Hills Sports Park anchors the area with baseball, softball and football fields and a looped running trail, and Rail Ranch Elementary is within walking distance for many residents. Real estate profiles put it about four miles from Old Town Murrieta and about two miles from the shopping centers along the 215 freeway.

Housing is a mix. Profiles describe Mediterranean-style homes built from the 1980s to the mid-2010s, with a median build year around 2003, alongside townhomes, some mobile homes and custom or renovated homes on multi-acre lots. Los Alamos Hills does not appear as a specific plan on the city zoning map, and published sources do not give a boundary, so we use Los Alamos Road and the sports park as anchors and confirm your address by phone.
